About Me

I am Mengzhen Li, a computer science graduate from Xiamen University Tan Kah Kee College, China, where I earned a B.S. in Computer Science and Technology in 2026. My research background is in intelligent control, neural networks, and autonomous UAV systems.

As a co-author of a conference paper on neural-network-based UAV flight control, I have worked across algorithm development, simulation, software implementation, and system testing.

I am interested in robotics, autonomous systems, and machine learning for intelligent control.
